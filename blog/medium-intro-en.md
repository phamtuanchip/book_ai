# From Prompting to AI SDLC Architect: A Free Vietnamese Book on Using AI in Software Development

*A hands-on guide that goes beyond "using AI tools" to designing and governing an AI-driven development lifecycle.*

Most AI material stops at "here are some good prompts." This book aims higher. It moves quickly through the theory, spends most of its time on practical prompting and AI coding tools, then covers AI agents, and finally teaches how to apply AI across the **entire software development lifecycle** — including architecture, security and team governance. **It is written in Vietnamese today; an English edition is planned.**

The goal: after reading, you can *do* things, not only recognise the vocabulary.

## Who it is for

- Developers at any level who want AI to speed up daily work
- Students and career changers entering programming
- Team leads who want to bring AI into their team's process
- Engineers aiming to become an **AI SDLC Architect or Manager**: someone who designs, evaluates and governs agentic systems for a whole organisation

You need basic programming knowledge. You do not need any ML background.

## What is inside

The book has 26 chapters in four parts, plus a prompt-template library:

- **Part I — AI in a hurry:** what AI is and why now, how LLMs work (intuition, not maths), the model ecosystem and how to choose, limits and risks such as hallucination and privacy
- **Part II — Prompting and AI coding tools (the core):** prompting principles, advanced techniques (chain-of-thought, decomposition, self-critique), prompting for code, IDE tools such as Copilot and Cursor, working with CLI agents like Claude Code, evaluating AI output and avoiding uncontrolled "vibe coding," and a hands-on case study
- **Part III — Agents at work:** what an agent is, common agent architectures, building a simple agent, non-coding uses, and managing agent risk
- **Part IV — AI across the SDLC:** AI in planning and requirements, design and architecture, code/test/review/CI-CD, and operations and maintenance; then agentic SDLC architecture, its security, building an AI-native team, and the path from *applyer* to *architect*
- **Appendix — Prompt template library** for quick reference
